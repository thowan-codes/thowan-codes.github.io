## How I Got Here
I have a bachelor's degree in computer science and game design. Since school, I have worked on a variety of projects I will not name here, spent time working for a Minecraft Server called Legendary Hardcore (LHC for short).

Eventually, I found my way back into coding through Java and plugin development. I'm not the most experienced developer, but I know enough to get by, and I enjoy learning more with every project.

### AI Disclaimer
While I do use AI as a practical learning tool, it doesn't write a single line of code. It helps me plan, and troubleshoot, but more importantly, it helps me understand *why* something works. Or-more importantly, why it does not....

## Why Voices of the Void?
I found *Voices of the Void*, fell in love with it, finished a complete playthrough of the story, and eventually decided to try the modded experience.

While looking through available mods, I found gaps in accessibility, quality-of-life, and more importantly content, that I wanted to see filled. That pushed me from being a player into developing mods myself.

Signals & Symbols is one results of that journey, and this Devlog is where I will share its progress alongside my other projects.
