## About Me

Hi, I’m Thowan!

### My Creative Side
Since I was 12 years old, I have written stories about dragons, magical creatures, and exploring the unknown. Over the years, my love of storytelling has evolved, it's morphed into running tabletop games with homebrewed worlds and creations. During that time, I also created a few YouTube channels. The main and latest one being [ThowanPlays](https://www.youtube.com/@ThowanPlays), which has primarily been dedicated to making gameplay videos on projects I want to look back on.

### My Development Journey
Back in 2014, I was told by several people that I should get into programming but no matter how much I studied, I always struggled to get the general gist of remembering all the different functions and terms. Then when the Covid Pandemic happened, just like everyone else, I was forced to stay home and inside. I decided to apply for college, and in a couple of years I had a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science focused on game design. That's of course right as the mass layoffs started, and other life events caused me to focus on other things.

Later, I ended up becoming an admin for a Minecraft Server called [Legendary Hardcore](https://wiki.legendaryhc.org/Main_Page). Through my experience as an admin, I got back into programming, and with the help of the new AI agents, was able to start teaching myself how to code in Java to make plugins for said server. After a few doxxing scares, I decided to step down and engage in other things. This ended up being Voices of the Void, which I instantly fell in love with and started creating mods for it, getting back to the roots of my game design degree!

### Outside of Development
Outside of my creative endeavors, I'm also a husband, a father, and someone who somehow manages to find more projects than I have time for! Between working full-time, spending time with my family, and managing and renovating rental properties, development is something I work on whenever life gives me the opportunity. Sometimes that means I can spend an entire weekend developing something, and other times it means a project has to sit untouched for weeks.

### About this website

It gives me a central location to update as well. I then later got involved in helping update the [VotV Modding Wiki](https://questwalker.github.io/votv-modding-wiki/) and Questwalker recommended I give this platform a try. So here we are!
