## What it is
An accessibility mod with other features included in it.

### Current Features
Displays a Level Indicator alongside the other signal info in the Player or Computer.

### Why I made it
I am colorblind, and the difference in colors between the Level 1, 2, and 3 signals was a little too hard for me to differentiate at a glance.
