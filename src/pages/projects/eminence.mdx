## What it is
Eminence is a content mod for Voices of the Void that aims to expand upon the game's mysterious nature through new items, events, anomalies, and signals for players to discover.

### Planned Features
- Unique items with their own behaviors and interactions.
- New events and encounters.
- Additional signals for players to discover.
- An expandable system for introducing new anomalies.

### Why I made it
One of the things I enjoy most about Voices of the Void is its sense of mystery. You never quite know what you might encounter or what significance something might have.

I wanted to explore creating my own content that contributes to that experience, while also giving myself an opportunity to experiment with game mechanics, storytelling, and event design.

## Current focus
- Preserve the atmosphere of Voices of the Void.
- Encourage curiosity and exploration.
- Create experiences that players can discover naturally.
- Avoid revealing too much about the content outside of the game.
- In case this is out of date, updates can typically be found under the GitHub [Issues](https://github.com/thowan-codes/Eminence/issues)
