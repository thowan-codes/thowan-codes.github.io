## What it is
Alpha Base Improvements is a mod for Voices of the Void focused on expanding the player's ability to improve, renovate, and personalize Alpha Base.

### Planned Feataures
- Custom models designed around the existing base architecture.

### Why I made it
Considering how much time players spend living and working within Alpha Base, I wanted to explore ways to make the base feel more useful to the player.

This project also gives me an opportunity to experiment with 3D modeling, environmental design, and integrating custom assets into an existing game world.

## Design Goals
- Maintain compatibility with the existing base layout.
- Preserve the original functionality of the base.
- Maintain the visual identity of Voices of the Void.
